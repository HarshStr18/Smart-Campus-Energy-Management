How SmartSave Works

Simple, unobtrusive automation that respects student routines while saving energy.

1

Occupancy Detected

When a student enters, the room's motion sensor identifies presence and automatically turns on configured lights and fans.

2

Room Becomes Empty

When students rush out to class or meals, the sensor detects that nobody is in the room anymore.

3

Grace Countdown Begins

A configurable countdown (e.g. 30 seconds) starts, ensuring devices aren't cut off if someone just stepped out for a moment.

4

Auto-Shutdown & Savings

If nobody returns, lights and fans automatically switch OFF. When someone returns later, power is instantly restored!

Our Design Thinking (AEIOU) Fieldwork Findings

Direct observations conducted during our campus audit that motivated the creation of SmartSave.

Location Type Observed Status Why It Happens (Root Cause) SmartSave Solution
Academic Classrooms (101-104) Lights & Fans Mostly OFF Scheduled lectures have professors and building staff who lock up and turn off switches when class ends. Maintains eco-standby; prevents accidental overnight usage if cleaning staff or late groups forget lights.
Hostel Rooms (201-203) Lights & Fans Frequently Left ON Students rush to 8:00 AM classes in a hurry. Fixed hostel fees mean students don't pay per-unit electric bills. Automatic grace countdown shuts off dorm lights and fans within minutes of departure.
Hostel Study Hall Lights & High-Speed Fans Left ON 24/7 Tragedy of the Commons: Late-night study groups leave without turning switches off, assuming someone else will. Autonomous shutoff eliminates continuous wasteful power consumption in shared campus spaces.
